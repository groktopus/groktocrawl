# W12.5 bounded specialist value experiment

## Question

When evidence obligations are genuinely separable, does a small set of typed
specialist handoffs improve the final research artifact enough to earn its
coordination cost?

The comparison scores final reconciled artifacts. Specialist count and prose
volume are not outcomes. Simple and non-separable cases must abstain from fan-out.

## Intervention

The control is one generalist artifact. The treatment assigns two fixed evidence
roles on independently labeled separable cases, validates their typed handoffs,
deduplicates canonical sources, preserves contradictory stances, and lets one lead
reconciler construct the final artifact. No specialist can publish or approve its
own evidence.

Both arms have the same three-call ceiling. The treatment may use two specialist
calls plus reconciliation; the control uses two calls. Non-separable treatment
reuses the control artifact and cost.

## Interpretation boundary

The corpus contains synthetic pinned evidence so the causal comparison is stable.
A blinded General-route reviewer scores usefulness and trustworthiness without arm
labels. This can justify a bounded separable-task pattern. It cannot establish a
generic swarm, automatic routing, real-world effect prevalence, or a particular
orchestration runtime.
