# W12.5 frozen protocol

Status: frozen before model review.

## Corpus and arms

Twelve cases cover two simple questions, one non-separable question, compound
questions, seeded contradictions, temporal questions, source identity, and
distinct evidence roles. Nine cases are independently marked separable before arm
construction. Each case runs three repetitions.

The control receives one generalist artifact. The treatment receives two typed
handoffs only on separable cases. Reconciliation validates assignment authority,
source acquisition, exact evidence identity, canonical duplication, conflict,
failure, and budget state. Simple and non-separable cases do not fan out.

## Reviewer

One paired review call sees artifacts labeled only A and B in a counterbalanced
order. The reviewer uses the pinned question and obligation IDs to score usefulness
and trustworthiness from 0 to 100. It is instructed to reward coverage and visible
conflict, not length or consensus. The route is local LiteLLM model alias `general`.

## Decision gate

Adopt only the separable-task pattern when at least two of three repetitions meet
every condition:

1. weighted evidence coverage improves by at least 10 percentage points;
2. blinded usefulness improves by at least 10 points;
3. no seeded contradiction is lost;
4. simple-task usefulness remains within 2 points of control;
5. total treatment calls remain within 50% of control;
6. no hard handoff, authority, provenance, budget, or reviewer failure occurs.
