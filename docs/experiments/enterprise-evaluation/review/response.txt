Needs revision.

P1 blockers for this design packet

- Corpus leakage is acknowledged but not operationally prevented. The implementation agent, design reviewer, questions, source text, and proposed expectations are all co-visible. Correction: define separate tuning, calibration, and held-out artifacts with access controls; remove repeated dev/candidate questions; record exposure history and invalidate any exposed item.
- The 30-question future gate does not require 30 unique, non-template question instances. The current 42 cases contain repeated questions and six near-identical topic templates. Correction: define the ADR minimum as at least 30 unique held-out questions, with template families clustered and reported separately. Do not describe them as 30 independent enterprise workloads.
- “20 percent adverse contract cases” is not mapped to a denominator or operational definition. The manifest’s 24 negative/abstention cases are 57.1% of the 42 current cases, but it is unclear whether the ADR denominator is all cases, held-out questions, trials, or contract tests, and whether insufficient/source-dependence cases qualify as adverse contracts. Correction: define adverse categories, denominator, required count, and whether the constraint applies per arm and per topic.
- The packet does not define an executable authority-boundary test. “Do not call tools or claim approval” is presented as an answer requirement, but the corpus contains no tool interface, authorization decision, event trace, or side-effect oracle. Correction: split textual-policy judging from runtime contract testing, and specify observable tool-call, identity, approval, cancellation, retry, and side-effect records for the latter.
- The quality rubric has no complete aggregation or acceptance rule. It defines labels and severities but not how assertion-level labels become question-level scores, how partial coverage affects results, how citation failures interact with unsupported claims, or how critical findings block an arm. Correction: predeclare unit of analysis, scoring formulas, missing-data handling, and decision rules before outputs are collected.
- Proposed expectations are explicitly not ground truth, but the packet still relies on them as the apparent reference structure. Correction: independently adjudicate a calibration set against the source bundles, preserve disagreement and uncertainty, and prohibit author expectations from serving as gold labels without adjudication.

P2 blockers for future comparative execution

- A/B and B/C separation is stated but not specified. The packet does not define the arms, estimands, policy intervention, runtime intervention, or which variables must remain constant in each comparison. Correction: add an ADR-aligned comparison matrix identifying the sole changed factor, paired unit, analysis population, and non-combinable comparisons.
- The zero-violation invariants lack test coverage and denominators. “Zero tested” does not say how many authorization attempts, secret exposures, ambiguous writes, cancellations, or provenance transitions were exercised. Correction: publish contract-test inventories and denominators by invariant, including failure-path coverage; treat zero observed violations as bounded evidence, not proof of zero risk.
- The five stochastic trials per arm requirement is present, but the analysis plan does not specify randomization, seed handling, trial independence, retry policy, cache/warm-state controls, or treatment of correlated failures. Correction: freeze these rules, retain all raw trials, and report both question-level paired results and trial distributions.
- The 30 runtime repetitions requirement is not tied to a defined workload or denominator. “Per runtime workload” needs a workload catalog, task identity, stopping rule, timeout treatment, and cost/latency measurement boundary. Correction: define workload fixtures and distinguish task failures, harness failures, provider failures, and timeouts.
- Cost and resource comparisons remain underspecified. The packet says to freeze limits but does not define token accounting, retries, cache effects, queue time, warm-up, hardware, or provider billing source. Correction: specify an auditable measurement boundary and normalization method before B/C execution.
- Calibration is correctly marked as not yet performed and preserves the one-human limitation, but the packet does not define the minimum calibration protocol or what constitutes a “calibration failure.” Correction: predeclare sampled strata, disagreement categories, adjudication procedure, judge changes permitted after calibration, and the rule that quality/adoption conclusions remain provisional under the one-human limitation.

The proposal is honest about its current corpus not being held out, the absence of results, unset performance thresholds, and lack of human labels. It is design-ready only for rubric and harness preparation. It is not ready for comparative execution or quality/adoption claims until the P1 controls, denominators, authority-boundary observables, and A/B/B/C estimands are specified.
