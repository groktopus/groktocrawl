# W12.4 research log

## 2026-09-19 — freeze

The protocol, 12-case replay corpus, policy implementation, runner, analyzer, and
decision thresholds were committed before the measurement. W12.1's negative result
was carried forward: obligation profiles are narrow experiment inputs rather than
a mandatory universal mission schema.

## 2026-09-19 — measurement

The frozen run completed 108 records: 12 cases, three arms, and three repetitions.
All repetitions produced identical arm-level scores, as expected from a
deterministic replay; shuffle affects execution order, not candidate content.

The treatment passed the preregistered gate in every repetition. Its only zero-gain
continuation was the intentionally unanswerable case, after which it stopped. The
generic diagnostic spent a follow-up query in every case without closing the
constructed obligation set.

## Decision

Adopt the ledger and stopping contract only behind the experimental boundary.
Retain fixed retrieval as the stable default until a live search calibration shows
that obligation authoring and obligation-specific retrieval are accurate enough
to earn their cost.

## 2026-09-19 — CI correction and clean rerun

CI found a static type error from reusing the name `query` for an optional lookup.
The local was renamed without changing behavior, the policy digest was refreshed,
and the full deterministic measurement was rerun from the corrected freeze.
