# W12.4 frozen protocol

Status: frozen before the three-repetition measurement run.

## Corpus

Twelve synthetic replay cases cover easy stop, missing primary evidence,
freshness, source duplication, contradiction, entity identity, low initial rank,
and an unanswerable obligation. Candidate acquisition, derivation, publisher,
canonical identity, materiality, and gold closure relations are fixed in
`w12.4-cases.json`.

The synthetic construction is deliberate. The experiment changes only the
continuation policy while holding candidate availability constant. A later live
retrieval calibration is required before claiming real-world prevalence or gain.

## Execution

- Three repetitions.
- Seed `20260919`; arm/case order is shuffled and retained.
- Shared limit of three queries per case.
- No model call and no network call. This avoids provider variance in a policy
  contract test.
- Score final admitted evidence against the frozen obligation closure rules.

## Primary gate

The obligation arm passes a repetition only when all conditions hold:

1. challenge-stratum weighted closure improves by at least 10 percentage points
   over fixed retrieval;
2. unsupported importance-3 obligations do not increase;
3. admitted precision remains within 5 percentage points of fixed retrieval;
4. no more than 10% of its executed queries occur after the last material gain.

Adopt the control only for an experimental path if at least two of three
repetitions pass. The easy-stop cases must incur no continuation. The unanswerable
case must stop after its first zero-gain attempt. Any unbounded loop, unknown
identity, or closure without required admitted evidence invalidates the run.
