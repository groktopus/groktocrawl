# W12.2 Research Thread experiment brief

## What we are testing

When someone returns to a research question days or months later, a fresh answer
can miss what changed or quietly treat an old fact as current. W12.2 tests whether
an explicit history of sources, claims, corrections, and open questions produces a
better update than starting over.

The proposed thread is deliberately modest. It connects complete research records;
it does not replace them, decide which claim is true, or turn prior prose into
evidence.

## What success would mean

The treatment must make follow-up answers more accurate and useful while reducing
repeated work. It must never merge a similar but different subject, erase history,
or describe stale evidence as current. Efficiency without those safety properties
does not count as success.

## What this milestone delivers first

The first milestone freezes the comparison and supplies a machine-checked thread
record. The next milestones add the longitudinal corpus, matched runner, blinded
grader, analysis, retained evidence packet, and an ADR recording the result.
