# W12.2 Research Thread research log

This log records every measured launch, exclusion, and bounded correction for the
longitudinal Research Thread comparison. Times are UTC.

## V1 generation and grading

V1 completed all nine initial roots and all 54 matched follow-up trials. The blind
grading lane completed 51 grades and failed three, inside its operational
guardrail. The pass is excluded because the grader prompt did not state that the
five score fields used a 0-to-100 scale. Valid responses mixed proportions and
percent scores, and the frozen analyzer omitted incomplete pairs.

Before any correction, the complete 235-file v1 evidence tree was retained with a
26,281-byte canonical manifest whose SHA-256 digest is
`cd657e3f50c323b76952bb9133939c3d7705e67aa0e5be4b6ca7c216c75fd876`.

## V2 scale correction

V2 inherited the exact v1 generation records and made no new generation calls. It
explicitly anchored every score from 0 through 100 and conservatively retained all
27 allocated pairs in analysis. It completed 44 grades and failed 10. The 18.5%
terminal grading failure rate exceeded the frozen below-10% guardrail, so v2 is
excluded in full.

The 44 valid records confirmed the scale correction. Of the 10 failures, seven
were non-final completions and three used a different grading template. The final
v2 tree contains 463 files. Its 55,738-byte canonical manifest has SHA-256 digest
`991d2353352cc5bf159e799ed687b194f9cd234545deffe39de21b3790143651`.

## V3 preflight and bounded correction

The v3 preflight used only the 10 candidates that failed v2 and remained outside
the measured tree. An exact output example and field definitions produced valid
grades for the eight candidates that returned within the first preflight window.
One of the remaining candidates exhausted 12,000 completion tokens entirely in
hidden reasoning and returned no grade. A later attempt encountered a temporary
transport timeout. With both bounded transport retry and one invalid-completion
retry enabled, all 10 preflight candidates produced valid grades.

V3 will reuse the exact v1 generation records, regrade all 54 candidates, retain
every raw grading attempt, and apply the frozen retry policy uniformly. It will not
reuse a v1 or v2 grade.

## V3 final result

V3 completed 52 grades and retained two terminal transport failures, a 3.7%
failure rate inside the frozen operational guardrail. The frozen conservative
assignments produced a -9.0-point mean treatment effect. Excluding the two
incomplete pairs, the treatment still averaged 2.8 points worse across 25 fully
observed pairs. It was 23.0% slower, used 51.2% more model tokens, and produced two
stale-current leaks in completed contradiction grades. No repetition reached the
required 10-point improvement. The tested default Research Thread is rejected.

The validated v3 tree contains 409 files and 7,421,004 content bytes. Its
49,389-byte canonical content-digest-plus-relative-path manifest has SHA-256
`aaee94a4e3786f9d0d3580a0aca552aaff910d57bb77a1550ea27a58fa47395a`.
