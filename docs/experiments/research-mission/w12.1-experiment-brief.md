# W12.1 Research Mission experiment brief

Status: **preregistered; measurement has not started**

## Decision

This experiment decides whether an explicit Research Mission contract should be
used for all research, only for difficult requests, revised and retested, or
rejected. It does not authorize a mainline rewrite or make GroktoCrawl X a
replacement for mainline GroktoCrawl.

The risky assumption is that turning a request into explicit obligations before
search improves the user's eventual decision. A schema that is internally tidy
but does not improve the research result fails this experiment.

## Hypothesis

- **Independent variable:** enforcement of a typed Research Mission before
  acquisition and synthesis.
- **Primary outcomes:** weighted obligation coverage, scope violations, and a
  blinded decision-usefulness judgment.
- **Predicted direction:** mission enforcement improves difficult-case coverage
  and reduces scope violations without materially harming straightforward work.
- **Smallest useful effect:** on ambiguous and compound cases, at least a
  10-point coverage improvement and a 30% relative reduction in scope
  violations in at least two of three repetitions.
- **Null:** the contract adds ceremony, latency, or over-constraint without a
  practically useful improvement.

## Method

Use a matched technical comparison rather than an A/B product rollout. Twelve
frozen cases cover six strata: straightforward, ambiguous, compound, temporal,
contradictory-source, and unanswerable. Every case runs three times in each arm.
Arm order is counterbalanced within case and repetition.

Two linked comparisons separate the possible sources of value:

1. **Downstream isolation:** both arms receive equivalent independently authored
   mission semantics. The control receives them as prose through the incumbent
   path; the treatment receives the typed contract and enforces its obligations.
2. **Intake accuracy:** the normalizer receives only the raw request. Its output
   is compared with a sealed reference mission. Clarification behavior is
   measured separately from downstream research quality.

This is the lightest method that can causally isolate contract enforcement. A
user interview cannot establish research completeness, while a production
rollout would expose users before the boundary is shown to help.

## Population and bounds

- Domain: enterprise agentic-engineering software factories.
- Cases: 12, with two cases in each stratum.
- Repetitions: three per arm for downstream comparison; three normalization
  attempts per raw request for intake evaluation.
- Model route: the pinned `general` LiteLLM alias, recorded with the provider's
  returned model identity when available.
- Acquisition: the same SlopSearX endpoint, source corpus, result cap, scraper,
  and search budget for both downstream arms.
- Concurrency: at most five model-bearing trials at once; reduce on rate limit,
  gateway instability, or memory pressure without changing arm allocation.
- Per-case ceiling: 10 acquired sources, four searches, six model calls, and 300
  elapsed seconds. Every actual call and elapsed duration is retained.

## Measures

Weighted obligation coverage is closed obligation weight divided by total
obligation weight. A material claim is supported only by admissible evidence
linked to that claim. A scope violation is an unsupported expansion, an omitted
hard scope boundary, or authority attributed to the requester without evidence.
Decision usefulness is a blinded ordinal judgment of whether the result supports
the stated decision, with the rationale retained.

Secondary measures are clarification and correction burden, appropriate
abstention, reproducibility, mission-field completeness, unnecessary fields,
latency, calls, tokens, estimated cost, and straightforward-case regression.
Structure, semantic quality, and user usefulness are reported separately.

## Guardrails and stopping rules

The run stops and is invalid if treatment-only facts leak into acquisition, a
reference mission changes after outputs are visible, an arm receives different
research inputs, or a result cannot be reconstructed from retained evidence.
One hard boundary failure blocks adoption: invented authority, silent scope
broadening, loss of a freshness requirement, or an unauthorized action.

Operationally, stop new launches if the trial failure rate exceeds 10%, p95
latency exceeds the matched control by more than 25%, or the provider shows
sustained rate-limit or gateway failures. Preserve every interrupted run before
resuming. Stop for futility after two repetitions only if the maximum possible
third repetition cannot satisfy the frozen gate.

The corpus contains no private customer data or vulnerable-population records.
Only the minimum prompts, source excerpts, model receipts, and grades needed to
reconstruct the result are retained.

## Decision rule

Adopt the contract for the tested difficult strata only when the practical
effect above is met in at least two repetitions, decision usefulness is no more
than two points worse on the 100-point scale in any anchor stratum, cost and p95
latency each rise no more than 25% unless the retained review explicitly judges
the excess worthwhile, and no hard boundary failure occurs.

Universal adoption additionally requires no material regression on
straightforward cases. Otherwise choose selective adoption, revision and a new
test, or rejection. A positive intake score cannot rescue a downstream failure,
and polished prose cannot rescue missing obligations.

The experiment owner is the GroktoCrawl X maintainer. The automated analysis may
recommend a decision, but the retained evidence and case-level grades remain the
reviewable basis for that decision.

## Required durable artifacts

- frozen case corpus, reference missions, analysis plan, prompts, and hashes;
- normalized missions and clarification traces;
- control and treatment execution traces and model receipts;
- source, claim, evidence, contradiction, exclusion, and unresolved-obligation
  records;
- blinded grades, adjudications, failed-run ledger, and sensitivity analysis;
- cost and latency accounting;
- outcome report and roadmap/ADR update.

No essential evidence may exist only in chat or an untracked temporary file.
