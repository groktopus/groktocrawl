# W12.3 independent claim-verification protocol

Status: **complete; bounded verifier passed default-adoption gates**

## Comparison

- **Control:** use the frozen incumbent publication decision for the source-bound
  claim. This represents a claim that has passed structural citation and
  provenance checks without a new semantic verifier.
- **Treatment:** apply one blinded independent verifier, then publish only when it
  returns `supported`, confidence of at least 70, and a traceable supporting span.

The verifier uses the thin local LiteLLM `general` route. It did not generate the
frozen claims, control decisions, or reference judgments. This establishes
information and decision-path independence without paying for a full agent shell.
The exact returned model identity and route receipt are retained for every call.

The independent variable is the semantic verification step. Claim text, evidence,
risk, obligations, expected judgment, repetitions, and scoring remain fixed.

## Corpus

Freeze at least 12 cases and three repetitions covering direct support, fluent but
unsupported claims, missing evidence, ignored contradictions, correct abstention,
stale sources, derivative-only corroboration, ambiguous identity, hostile source
instructions, and high-consequence claims. At least four cases must be truly
publishable so indiscriminate refusal is visible. At least three false-accept
opportunities must be high risk.

Every reference judgment is authored from the exact spans before execution. The
corpus records required spans, correct verdict, correct publication decision, and
whether a false acceptance is critical. Expected judgments never enter verifier
prompts.

## Primary measurements

- overall and high-risk false supported-claim rate;
- critical false accepts;
- missed contradictions;
- false rejection of publishable claims;
- inappropriate abstention;
- evidence-span alignment;
- Brier score for support calibration.

Report repetitions independently and together. Retain invalid responses as
failures. A missing treatment result is conservatively scored as publishing a
non-publishable claim and rejecting a publishable claim.

## Cost and latency

Record calls, tokens, cost, and latency for every verifier attempt. Control has no
incremental verification call. The treatment budget is one valid verifier
completion per candidate, at most two bounded transport attempts, and one fresh
completion after an invalid response. Five candidates may run concurrently.

For high-risk selective adoption, verifier p95 latency must remain at or below 120
seconds and mean incremental model calls must remain at or below 1.2 per claim.
Default verification must also show value in the low-risk stratum; otherwise only
the high-risk route is eligible.

## Frozen decision rule

Adopt selective high-risk verification only if, in at least two of three
repetitions:

- critical false accepts fall by at least 30%;
- missed contradictions fall;
- false rejection and inappropriate abstention each rise by no more than 10
  percentage points relative to control;
- Brier score improves;
- there is no publication-authority, input-contamination, provenance, or hostile
  instruction-following failure;
- the high-risk cost and latency ceilings pass.

Adopt default verification only if those gates also pass in aggregate and the
low-risk false-accept reduction is at least 20% without additional false
rejection. Otherwise retain the current path or revise the verifier boundary.

## Stop and exclusion rules

Stop before measurement if the verifier receives a reference label, control
decision, hidden generator rationale, or arm identity. Exclude an entire launch if
more than 10% of candidates end in terminal verifier failure, if case allocation
changes after calls begin, or if a parser repairs semantic content. Preserve every
excluded launch and publish its reason in the research log.

Transport retries may repeat an identical request. A schema-invalid or non-final
completion may receive one fresh attempt under the frozen policy; both raw attempts
must be retained. No selective rerun may replace a terminal measured result.

### Visible v1 amendment

V1 was stopped after 24 of 36 allocated trials because four terminal validation
failures made its final failure rate irreversibly exceed 10%. All four occurred in
contradiction-bearing cases. Three exposed a mismatch: the JSON schema allowed a
span to appear in the general cited-evidence list and its contradiction subset,
while the deeper validator incorrectly required the two lists to be disjoint. One
candidate twice returned a different output template. V1 is retained and excluded.

V2 makes `contradiction_span_ids` an explicit subset of `evidence_span_ids`, while
still requiring uniqueness within each list and local evidence identities. The
prompt includes the exact compact output object and field relationship. The corpus,
references, work order, model route, scoring, budgets, and decision gates remain
unchanged. V2 uses a new result tree and does not repair or reuse v1 outputs.
