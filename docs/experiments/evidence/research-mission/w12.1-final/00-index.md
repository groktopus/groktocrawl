# W12.1 Research Mission evidence

This packet contains the secret-free public evidence for the frozen W12.1
comparison. The full private provider envelopes and prompts remain on
`inference.example.internal`; their response and envelope digests are retained in the public
receipts and were replay-validated before analysis.

## Result

The universal typed Research Mission is rejected for the tested enterprise
agentic-software-factory domain. It passed the difficult-case practical-effect
gate in one of three repetitions. Coverage fell by 12.5 percentage points in
the other two. Independent regrading did not recover a treatment advantage.

Intake normalization was the larger failure: 40% action accuracy, mean required
field recall of 2.9/100, 11 hard-boundary failures, and 94 required correction
actions across 35 completed blind grades. The treatment did pass the operational
and downstream hard-boundary gates, and it did not regress straightforward-case
decision usefulness. Those facts are insufficient for adoption.

This experiment tested one domain, one model route, 12 cases, and three
repetitions. It rejects this universal contract and normalizer; it does not show
that smaller internal state objects, user-authored constraints, or contracts
triggered only for named complex workflows lack value.
