# W12.5 outcome

The specialist treatment did the mechanical work correctly. It covered all three
obligations on the separable cases, retained exact source and evidence identities,
preserved both seeded contradictions, avoided fan-out on simple cases, and used
1.36–1.41 times the control's calls.

That did not become a reliable improvement in the judged final artifact. The
blinded reviewer preferred treatment in 29 of 33 completed pairs, but the measured
usefulness gain was below the required 10 points in every repetition. The reviewer
also used the declared 0–100 scale inconsistently, sometimes calling an artifact
maximal while assigning zero, and three responses remained structurally incomplete
after bounded retries. Those are hard evaluation failures under the frozen gate.

The result does not say specialists can never help. It says this experiment did
not earn generic fan-out. The reusable finding is the typed handoff and lead
reconciliation boundary: assignment authority, exact evidence spans, source
identity, conflict preservation, budget state, and visible failure all survived.
A future experiment needs a real complex-task corpus, calibrated independent
reviewers, and a narrower task class before specialist execution is promoted.
